FAQ

Common questions

Is there a free version?
Yes. Free accounts get 10 questions per day with full access to practice, explanations, and progress tracking. Pro unlocks unlimited questions, AI study plans, and timed test modes.
Are the questions real SAT questions?
The app pulls from OpenSAT, a database of publicly released College Board questions, and supplements with AI-generated questions tuned to real SAT difficulty and format.
Can I use it without internet?
Yes. SAT Navigator includes 320+ offline questions across all 32 topics. If your connection is slow or unavailable, it automatically falls back to the local question bank.
What if I switch devices?
Save your progress to the cloud with your email in Settings. Restore it on any device with the same email — no account or password needed.
Can I cancel anytime?
Yes, cancel anytime with no penalty. Your Pro access continues until the end of the billing period.
How is this different from Khan Academy?
Khan Academy is broad and general. SAT Navigator is built specifically for score improvement — it tracks your predicted score, tells you exactly what to practice next, tags your error patterns, and simulates real test conditions.
